Create a professional AI/RAG system architecture diagram that closely matches the following design.

CANVAS AND STYLE
- Use a 16:9 landscape canvas.
- Use a very dark navy-to-black background.
- Use a modern cybersecurity/AI architecture style.
- Use rounded rectangular section borders with subtle matching-color glow.
- Use white text and simple white outline icons.
- Keep every component as an independent shape.
- Do not use swimlanes, tables, or expandable containers.
- Preserve all wording exactly.
- Use straight and curved connectors without allowing lines to cross through components.
